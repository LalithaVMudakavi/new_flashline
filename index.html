01

Four services.
One accountable partner.

Most products pass through four hands before they ship. Ours pass through one roof — which is why they ship on time.

S/01

Engineering Design

Expert CAM engineers translate your schematics into manufacturable reality — PCB layout, DFM/DFA review, and electromechanical design that anticipates the production line instead of fighting it.

  • CAM engineering
  • DFM / DFA
  • Electromechanical design
S/02

PCB Fabrication

Prototype to production runs of rigid, rigid-flex, metal-core and HDI boards — up to 60 layers — backed by first-article inspection on every new part number.

  • 60-layer HDI
  • Rigid-flex
  • Metal-core
  • Prototype → volume
S/03

PCB Assembly

Two automated Fuji Aimex II SMT lines place components from 01005 chips to 75 mm BGAs at 30-micron accuracy, with 3D optical inspection, X-ray, and nitrogen reflow in-house.

  • 01005 → 75 mm BGA
  • SMD + mixed technology
  • 3D AOI + X-ray
S/04

Box Build

Complete product integration — enclosures, cable harnesses, firmware loading, functional and environmental test, and packaging. Your product arrives ready to sell, not ready to assemble.

  • Full integration
  • Functional test
  • Turnkey delivery

04

Industry 4.0,
on an actual factory floor.

Every board is digitally tracked from kit to crate. You see what we see.

  1. 01 — Quote & DFM

    Send your files; our CAM team returns a manufacturability review with the quote — before you commit.

  2. 02 — Sourcing

    A global supply chain spanning Singapore, USA, Taiwan, Korea and China keeps components moving even when markets don't.

  3. 03 — First article

    Every new part number passes FAI with X-ray and 3D optical inspection before volume begins.

  4. 04 — Production

    Automated SMT lines with digital lot tracking — live status, full traceability, zero guesswork.

  5. 05 — Test & ship

    Functional, burn-in, and environmental testing, then box-build and packaging. Product ships market-ready.
